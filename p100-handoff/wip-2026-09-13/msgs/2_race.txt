fix: GEMM attention softmax writes probabilities out of place -- the in-place write raced

Since 0f5b88954 the softmax wrote P over the scores S it had just read, in one buffer. On this
toolchain the store can land before the load of the same element -- with the two aliased
__restrict__ parameters of the original, with one restrict pointer, and with no restrict at
all. A probability (<= 1/8) read back as a score gives exp(4P - m) against a very negative row
max: |dP| up to 1.6e7 on the affected rows. Silent in fp32; in fp16 it overflows half and NaNs
the output.

Found through run-to-run variation of fp32 perplexity on identical input (3.3144-3.3272 on one
16k chunk). Isolated: tile path deterministic; CUDA_LAUNCH_BLOCKING=1 still varied; cuBLAS alone
bit-identical; per-call hashes showed a call with bit-identical inputs and a different output;
per-stage checksums put the divergence in the softmax's second pass. Proven with an in-op
self-check running the kernel twice on identical inputs (fp32, 16k, ~2240 launches per run):
in place 4, 6 and 3 mismatches (restrict, restrict, no restrict); out of place 0 of ~6700.
fp32 now reads 3.3165 on every run (10 runs). fp16 3.3185 every run.

Cost: ~1% of the attention op, and nkv_c*nt*gqa more elements of scratch (50 MB per GPU at
-ub 2048 in fp16).

Also in this change:
- GGML_CUDA_FA_GEMM_PREC=32: fp32 accumulation for both GEMMs over the same fp16 K and Q (exact
  products), fp32 scores, probabilities and V. NMSE vs CPU fp32 1.5e-6, against 2.3e-6 for the
  tile kernel. -11% pp2048 at depth 16384, -27% at 65536. Default unchanged.
- decline K/V with nb[0] != ggml_type_size(type): the strided dequantizers assume it, as
  upstream asserts before the same call (fattn-common.cuh:1036).
