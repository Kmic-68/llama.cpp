fix: decide peer-copy compression per exchange from the matmul's compute type

A tensor-parallel partial is shipped as f16 only when its values are f16-exact. That was decided
once per run: a probe checks the first eligible exchange, and every later MUL_MAT output of >= 512
rows is then compressed. Whether a matmul's output is f16-exact depends on that matmul, though.
Quantized or F16 weights take the COMPUTE_16F cuBLAS path and are exact; F32 or BF16 weights, and
matmuls that request GGML_PREC_F32 (GLM4, GLM4_MOE and JAIS2 set it on exactly these output
projections), produce genuine f32. In a model that mixes the two, the f32 partials were silently
rounded to f16 whenever the first exchange happened to be exact.

The compute-type decision of ggml_cuda_mul_mat_cublas moves into
ggml_cuda_mul_mat_cublas_compute_type, and eligibility now requires F16 from it. The probe stays
as the second guard.

For Qwen3.8-27B Q6_K nothing changes: every matmul whose output is all-reduced -- attn_output,
ffn_down, ssm_out, nextn.eh_proj -- is Q6_K, and qwen35 requests no fp32 precision, so the
eligibility of every exchange, the output bits and the speed are as before. The F32 tensors in that
file are norms, biases, ssm_a and the conv1d weight, none of which is a matmul weight.
