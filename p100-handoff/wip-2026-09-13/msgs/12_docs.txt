The headline for anyone reading this later: **the only measurable accuracy gap between this fork
and an all-fp32 run is the prefill matmuls' fp16 inputs and accumulation** -- paired per-chunk over
4096 x 30 tokens, fp16 matmuls sit +0.0034 nats/token from fp32 (t 7.4) and fp32 matmuls sit at
-0.0003 (t -1.4, i.e. indistinguishable). `GGML_CUDA_CUBLAS_COMPUTE_TYPE=f32` buys that back for
~40% of prefill throughput and nothing at all on decode. The default stays fp16 + ALGO6.
